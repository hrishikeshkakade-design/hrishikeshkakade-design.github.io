Case study·Batchwise·Health & nutrition

Meal prep, planned for the week, not the plate.

Batchwise is an assistant for people who cook in batches. It plans high-protein nutrition around your diet, keeps track of the ingredients you have, builds recipes for the equipment in your kitchen, and tracks what you eat against what you burn.
