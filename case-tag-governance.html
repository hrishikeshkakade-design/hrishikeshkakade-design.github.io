02 · Problem

Name the problem in one line

Cost allocation only works if resources are tagged with an owner, team and environment. In practice tags were missing, inconsistent or misspelled, and fixing them meant hunting through thousands of resources by hand.

03 · Research

What we learned

How did you research this? Interviews, data, competitor review, support tickets…

Insight

Nobody owns tagging

Platform teams set the rules, but the people who create resources rarely know them.

Insight

Small typos break reports

"prod", "Prod" and "production" split one environment into three in every cost report.

Insight

Fixing one by one does not scale

Accounts had thousands of resources, so any fix had to work in bulk.
