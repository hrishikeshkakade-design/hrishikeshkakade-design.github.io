Design philosophy

Good product design is as much about making the right decisions as it is about creating the right interfaces.

It's about understanding the problem beneath the request, questioning assumptions and navigating constraints. The best solutions balance what users need, what the business is trying to achieve and what teams can realistically build.

  • 0 to 1 ownershipHelped establish OneLens from its early stages as Astuto.ai's first product designer.
  • Complex productsExperience across cloud cost management, AI observability, cost allocation and fintech.
  • Systems thinkingConnecting workflows, data models, design systems and engineering into cohesive experiences.
  • Cross-functional partnershipWorking closely with founders, product and engineering from early problem definition through execution.

How I approach product design

I bring structure to ambiguous problems, connect user needs with business goals, and work across design and engineering to turn ideas into useful, scalable products. My approach balances thoughtful exploration with practical execution, ensuring that what we design works beyond the Figma canvas.
